Statue of Liberty Tours: What to Book Before You Arrive

The important choice is the access level, ferry plan and time commitment—not simply ticket versus tour.

What to compare

  • Ferry ticket inclusion
  • Pedestal or crown access
  • Ellis Island inclusion
  • Guided vs self-guided format
  • Departure point

How to decide

Compare the total price, what is actually included, cancellation terms, travel time and fit for your trip. Re-check live merchant terms before paying.

Before booking

  • Confirm the date, meeting point or check-in rules.
  • Read cancellation and refund conditions.
  • Check taxes, resort fees, baggage or other add-ons.
  • Save the final booking confirmation and terms.

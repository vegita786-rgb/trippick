7-Day Dog Enrichment Rotation

A simple one-week rotation so your dog gets variety without needing expensive gear.

Example rotation

  1. Day 1: sniff-focused walk + 3 minutes of targeting.
  2. Day 2: food-search game.
  3. Day 3: basic obedience refresh + toy-name practice.
  4. Day 4: cardboard-box search.
  5. Day 5: short hide-and-seek.
  6. Day 6: calm impulse-control practice.
  7. Day 7: easy review of the two activities your dog enjoyed most.

Keep sessions short and adjust difficulty to your dog's age, mobility and comfort.

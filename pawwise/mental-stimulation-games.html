7 Simple Mental-Stimulation Ideas for Dogs

Low-cost enrichment ideas you can rotate without buying lots of equipment.

Food-search game

Scatter part of a normal meal in a safe area and let your dog use scent to find it.

Cardboard-box search

Use clean boxes with treats or toys hidden safely inside or between them.

Basic targeting

Teach your dog to touch a hand or target with the nose, then build short repetitions.

Toy-name practice

Repeat one or two toy names consistently and reward correct choices.

Short hide-and-seek

Have a family member hide nearby and call the dog for a simple search game.

Impulse-control pause

Practice brief waits before meals, doors or toy play, keeping sessions easy.

Sniff walk

Allow extra time for safe sniffing instead of turning every walk into distance training.

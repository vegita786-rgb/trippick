Online Dog Training Course Checklist

Five things to compare before paying for a self-paced dog-training course.

1. Trainer credentials

Look for clearly stated professional qualifications and transparent background information.

2. Teaching method

Check whether the course explains its training approach and whether that approach matches how you want to work with your dog.

3. Format

Confirm whether you get videos, written steps, troubleshooting help and mobile-friendly access.

4. Support

See whether questions can be submitted and what kind of support is actually included.

5. Refund terms

Read the current guarantee directly on the checkout or sales page.

Dog Barking & Boredom Checklist

A calm checklist to work through before assuming one trick will solve excessive barking.

Check the context

  • When does the barking happen?
  • What happens immediately before it?
  • Is the dog under-stimulated, over-stimulated, frightened or reacting to a trigger?
  • Does the dog get enough age-appropriate movement, sniffing and mental activity?
  • Are you accidentally rewarding barking with attention or access?

Keep a 3-day log

Write down time, trigger, duration and what stopped the barking. Patterns are easier to spot on paper than from memory.

Times Square Hotel Fees Checklist: What to Check Before Paying

A simple checklist for spotting destination fees, parking costs, breakfast charges and other extras before you book a Times Square hotel.

Check these before booking

  • Mandatory destination or facility fees
  • Taxes shown only at checkout
  • Parking cost if you have a car
  • Breakfast inclusion vs paid breakfast
  • Cancellation deadline and prepaid-rate restrictions

Best for

Travelers comparing several Midtown/Times Square hotels where the headline nightly rate looks similar.

Watch out for

Two hotels with similar room rates can have very different final totals once mandatory extras are added.

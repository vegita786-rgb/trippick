## Travelpayouts project description
TripPick is an independent travel decision-guide website publishing original English-language content for travelers comparing hotels, tours, attraction tickets, day trips and airport transfers. The site focuses on practical purchase factors such as total cost, inclusions, cancellation terms, location, timing and traveler fit. Traffic is planned through organic search and free social distribution; no paid ads are planned at launch.

## Viator application description
TripPick publishes buyer-intent guides for travelers researching activities and attractions in major international destinations. Current content covers Rome, Paris, New York and Las Vegas, with additional destination guides added regularly. Viator links will be placed contextually inside relevant destination and activity guides rather than as unrelated banner spam.

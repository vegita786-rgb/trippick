Compare West Rim and South Rim day trips from Las Vegas by travel time, trip length and sightseeing priorities.

What matters most

Watch out for

Do not assume every Grand Canyon tour visits the same rim; the experience and travel time can be very different.

Booking checklist

  1. Compare the final price, not only the headline price.
  2. Check cancellation terms.
  3. Confirm what is actually included.
  4. Save the final confirmation and meeting/location details.

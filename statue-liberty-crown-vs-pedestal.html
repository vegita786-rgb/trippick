Statue of Liberty Crown vs Pedestal: Which Access Fits Your Visit?

A practical comparison checklist for choosing standard ferry access, pedestal access or crown access based on effort, time and what you actually want to see.

Check these before booking

  • Exact access level included
  • Stair/fitness requirements
  • Entry time and security rules
  • Ellis Island inclusion
  • Departure point and return timing

Best for

Travelers who already plan to visit Liberty Island but are unsure which access level is worth choosing.

Watch out for

Do not assume every tour listing includes pedestal or crown access; the exact access type should be stated clearly.

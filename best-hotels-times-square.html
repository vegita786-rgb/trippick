Best Hotels Near Times Square: How to Choose Without Overpaying

Times Square hotel prices can hide trade-offs in fees, noise, room size and transport access.

What to compare

  • Total nightly cost after destination or facility fees
  • Real walking distance to subway entrances
  • Room size and bed configuration
  • Street noise and high-floor availability
  • Cancellation terms

How to decide

Compare the total price, what is actually included, cancellation terms, travel time and fit for your trip. Re-check live merchant terms before paying.

Before booking

  • Confirm the date, meeting point or check-in rules.
  • Read cancellation and refund conditions.
  • Check taxes, resort fees, baggage or other add-ons.
  • Save the final booking confirmation and terms.
